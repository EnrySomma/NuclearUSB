1. BOILING (THE GOLD STANDARD)
Boiling is the most reliable method to kill all pathogens, including viruses,
bacteria, and protozoa (like Giardia and Cryptosporidium).
- Bring water to a rolling boil for at least 1 full minute.
- At altitudes above 2,000 meters (6,500 feet), boil for at least 3 minutes,
  as water boils at a lower temperature at high elevations.
- Let the water cool naturally before drinking.

2. CHEMICAL DISINFECTION
Chemical treatments are lightweight and convenient but require contact time.
- Iodine Tablets: Effective against bacteria and viruses. Poor against cryptosporidium.
  Add tablets per manufacturer's direction, shake, and wait 30 minutes.
- Chlorine Dioxide: Highly effective against all pathogens, including cryptosporidium.
  Requires up to 4 hours of treatment time depending on water temperature.
- Keep treated water away from direct sunlight during disinfection.

3. FILTRATION SYSTEMS
Filters remove suspended particles and micro-organisms based on pore size.
- Microfilters (0.1 - 0.2 microns): Remove bacteria and protozoa but not viruses.
  Requires pump or gravity flow.
- Purification Filters (down to 0.02 microns): Remove viruses as well.
- DIY Improvised Filter: Layer gravel, sand, crushed charcoal, and grass in a clean
  plastic bottle with a punctured cap. Pour turbid water through. This only removes
  sediment and large debris; you MUST boil or chemically treat the filtered water
  to make it safe to drink.

4. SOLAR DISINFECTION (SODIS METHOD)
If no other option exists, use solar radiation to disinfect water:
- Fill clear, clean PET plastic bottles with water.
- Place them horizontally on a dark metal sheet in direct, intense sunlight.
- Expose for at least 6 hours (or 2 days if cloudy).
- The UV-A radiation and increased temperature kill most biological pathogens.
